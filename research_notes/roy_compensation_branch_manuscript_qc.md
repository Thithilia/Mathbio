# Manuscript QC Note

## Main changes

- Polished the abstract to state the problem, model comparison, compensation mechanism, local stability, PDE spatial stability, non-homogeneous perturbation result, nonlinear trade-off extension, and final interpretation in a concise paper-style form.
- Tightened the introduction so it ends with explicit research aims and a conservative claim hierarchy.
- Updated spatial-mode notation from \(\lambda_{mn}\) to \(\lambda_{ij}\) in the manuscript text to avoid confusion with the predator mortality parameter \(m\).
- Expanded figure captions so each caption states what is plotted, why it matters, and what conclusion is allowed.
- Sharpened limitations and conclusion wording to avoid global or spatial-pattern-mediated claims.

## Claim audit

The manuscript now separates strong, moderate, and forbidden claims more explicitly.

Strong claims retained:

- the tested ODE has an interior compensation branch;
- the branch exists under explicit feasibility conditions;
- the current branch is locally stable by Routh-Hurwitz criteria at tested stresses;
- the current PDE branch is linearly stable to tested spatial modes;
- targeted non-homogeneous perturbations do not produce persistent spatial-pattern-mediated rescue;
- the nonlinear extension supports compensation branches beyond the linear case in a controlled local shape grid.

Moderate claims retained:

- the mechanism may extend beyond the linear trade-off form in selected nonlinear shapes;
- finite spatial heterogeneity can cause transient basin-boundary excursions.

Forbidden claims avoided:

- spatial structure causes rescue;
- spatial patterning drives rescue;
- spatial structure generally amplifies or suppresses rescue;
- the mechanism is globally stable;
- the nonlinear extension is fully general;
- all heterogeneous perturbations decay.

## Remaining weaknesses

- The manuscript is still a consolidated draft rather than a submission-ready paper.
- Several figures are diagnostic rather than journal-designed final panels.
- The nonlinear trade-off extension remains a controlled shape-grid result, not a theorem over nonlinear trade-offs.
- PDE perturbation tests remain targeted rather than exhaustive.
- The model remains uncalibrated to empirical predator-prey data.

## What would be needed before submission

- Decide the final journal target and adjust length, figure count, and formatting.
- Convert diagnostic panels into a smaller set of publication figures.
- Add a compact parameter table if the manuscript is submitted as a standalone article.
- Recheck every numerical value against the final CSVs before external circulation.
- Add only verified literature if the introduction or discussion is expanded.
